# Claude (claude.ai and Claude Desktop)

1. Open **Settings**, then **Connectors**.
2. Choose **Add custom connector**.
3. Name: `Prism`
4. URL: `https://prism.parad1gm.com/api/prism-mcp`
5. Add the connector, then connect it and sign in to your Prism account when prompted.

When you sign in, you choose what Claude may do:

- `prism:read` -- see your saved contracts, their deadlines and the risks Prism found.
- `prism:write` -- send contracts for Prism to read, using your credits.

To disconnect, remove the connector in Claude, or disconnect it from [Security](https://prism.parad1gm.com/prism/security) in your Prism account.
