# Claude (claude.ai and Claude Desktop)

The quickest way: find **Revalidata Contracts** in Claude's connector directory and connect it.

Or add it yourself:

1. Open **Settings**, then **Connectors**.
2. Choose **Add custom connector**.
3. Name: `Revalidata`
4. URL: `https://revalidata.com/mcp`
5. Add the connector, then connect it and sign in to your Revalidata account when prompted.

When you sign in, you choose what Claude may do:

- `prism:read`: see your saved contracts, their deadlines and the risks Revalidata found.
- `prism:write`: send contracts for Revalidata to read, using your credits.

To disconnect, remove the connector in Claude, or disconnect it from Security in your Revalidata account.
